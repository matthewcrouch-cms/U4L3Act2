Step 3: What do you notice?

Look back at the residual plots in each group. Compare where the points are relative to the 0 line (the x-axis).

Key idea

Good linear fit: the residuals are randomly scattered above and below the 0 line, with no pattern. The line is capturing the trend in the data.

Bad linear fit: the residuals show a pattern. They start above (or below) 0, then move below (or above) 0, and often come back again, forming a curve. A line is the wrong model for that data.
